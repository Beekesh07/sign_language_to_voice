# Sample clips

These short clips are from the **INCLUDE** Indian Sign Language dataset (AI4Bharat),
licensed under [CC-BY-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
They were obtained through the aggregated
[ISL Isolated Word Dataset (40 words)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/vidit031/isl-isolated-40words).

Sridhar et al., "INCLUDE: A Large Scale Dataset for Indian Sign Language Recognition", ACM Multimedia 2020.
